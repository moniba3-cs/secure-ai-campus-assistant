title: KOI Course Handbook

Assignment extension policy. Students may apply for an assignment extension of up to 7 days through the student portal. Applications must be lodged at least 24 hours before the assignment deadline and include supporting documents.

Assessment and grading. Assessments are graded on the scale HD, D, C, P and F. A pass requires at least 50 percent overall. Grades are released through the student portal after moderation.

Attendance. Students are expected to attend at least 80 percent of scheduled classes. Repeated absence may trigger an at-risk review by the course coordinator.
